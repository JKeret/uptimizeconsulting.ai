# Uptimize Consulting — 90-Day Marketing Plan (Design)

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Goal:** 1–2 signed projects at $10K–25K within 90 days, sourced through a repeatable pipeline rather than luck.
**Owner:** Jonathan (solo, alongside Aging Care 2B delivery). All client-facing copy in first-person singular ("I"), no em-dashes.

## Context

Every Uptimize deal to date came through warm referrals: Ronnie → Aging Care ($1K EOB parser → ~$50K platform), network → LaneFit ($2.5K + $1.5K/mo), IFM → Flyerly. The Aging Care arc — land with a small fixed-price project, expand into a platform — is the proven sales motion. This plan productizes that motion and runs it across five channels.

## 1. Core message

Pain-first: **"Every small business today runs on spreadsheets, paper, and duct-taped tools. A custom app — built in weeks, not months — optimizes the business and its processes."**

The pitch relates to the pain (manual billing, chasing paperwork, no visibility, double data entry) and arrives with a working solution, not a proposal deck. Proof points: Aging Care, LaneFit, Flyerly/IFM.

## 2. Offer ladder (land-and-expand)

The marketing sells the **wedge**, not the platform:

1. **$1K Starter Project** — "Pick your most annoying process. I'll automate it in about two weeks. Fixed price." (The EOB-parser move, productized.)
2. **Platform build** — $10K–25K, scoped after the starter project earns trust.
3. **Care plan** — $1.5K/mo retainer for hosting, changes, and support.

Rationale: nobody knows who needs a "custom platform," but everyone knows a business owner drowning in one annoying process. The wedge also makes the referral ask trivial.

## 4. Channels, tiered by expected yield

| Tier | Channel | Window | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Referral activation | Weeks 2–6 | List ~15–20 warm contacts (Ronnie, Ari, Guy, Allan, IFM, past clients). Personal note + one-pager + specific ask: "Who do you know drowning in one annoying manual process?" Grisha/LaneFit follow-up ships week 1 as part of this. |

| 3 | AEO / content | Background, weekly | Dogfood the dormant ai-visibility pipeline on uptimizeconsulting.ai. One article/week answering "custom app for [industry/process]" questions. |
| 4 | Paid experiment | Weeks 4+ | Facebook + Google, geo-targeted around Naperville, driving to the starter landing page. **$300/month, 60-day cap, killed if no qualified lead by day 30 of spend.** Treated as an experiment, not a pillar. |
| 4 | Local outreach | Opportunistic | Naperville-area businesses (IFM-style), in-person trust advantage. |

## 5. Tracking and cadence

- Pipeline lives in **Uptimize CRM** (dogfooding doubles as demo material). Every contact, conversation, and starter project is a CRM record.
- Weekly rhythm: Tier 1/2 touches batched into one outreach block; one content piece; review pipeline numbers.

## 6. Success criteria (90 days)

- 10+ qualified conversations
- 3+ paid $1K starter projects
- 1–2 starter projects converted to $10K+ platform builds
- Ad experiment produces a clear keep/kill verdict at ≤$600 total spend

## 7. Risks

- **Delivery collision:** 2B delivery and starter projects compete for the same hours. Mitigation: cap concurrent starter projects at 2.
- **Aging Care reference timing:** the full 2B story isn't publicly citable until Plan 9/BAA closes; case blurb stays anonymized and outcome-focused until then.
- **Cold paid traffic:** least-proven channel; bounded by budget and kill criteria above.

## 8. Out of scope

- Kitchen Table / Concordia marketing (parked pending Ari's AAA thinking).
- LaneFit productization beyond the Grisha follow-up.
- Public pricing beyond the $1K starter offer (platform pricing stays scoped-per-deal).
