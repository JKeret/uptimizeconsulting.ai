Replaced a 25-year-old billing spreadsheet with an automated tool that reads insurance remittances and posts payments line by line, then designed the full secure web platform to retire the spreadsheet entirely. Improved: eliminated hours of manual re-typing, cut posting errors, and moved a one-person-at-a-time file to a multi-user, compliant system.
