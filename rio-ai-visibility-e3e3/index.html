The shiftThe questions you used to win on Google are now answered by AI

When someone asks ChatGPT “Best commercial laundry for uniforms in Canoga Park, CA.”, it does not return ten blue links. It answers: names a handful of businesses, cites its sources, and moves on. There is no keyword bid into that answer. The organic discipline that decides who gets named is called AEO, and unlike most marketing, it is directly measurable.

What's actually buyable in AI ads today

Verified as of July 2026: OpenAI began testing ads in ChatGPT (US) in February 2026, shown only to free-tier users, clearly labeled, and per OpenAI they do not influence the organic answer. There is no open self-serve ad platform yet. Google's AI Mode carries ads through regular Google Ads. Bottom line: paid placement covers a sliver of AI answers; the main game is being the name in the organic answer.

Full methodology, and its honest limits

I wrote 36 questions phrased the way real people ask assistants, across 4 audiences (busy individual/family, self-service user, business client and brand checks). Each question went to ChatGPT and Gemini with web search enabled, 2 times each, to account for answer variability: 144 answers total, collected 2026-08-13.

Each answer was scored: was Rio Laundromat named? recommended? which competitors appeared? which websites did the AI cite as sources? The brand-name check is a literal text match, not an AI's opinion.

Limits, stated plainly: answers came through the models' developer interfaces with web search on, the closest measurable proxy to the consumer apps, but not byte-identical (no personal chat history). Answers also shift between runs and over time; that is why the plan includes re-measuring monthly rather than trusting any single snapshot, including this one.
